# Credits

## Background image
`assets/images/golden-gate-bridge.jpg` — Golden Gate Bridge at sunset, full span.

Resampled client-side (script.js) into a field of small dots. Dots whose
sampled pixel is dark, saturated red/orange (the bridge's painted steel, as
opposed to the sunset sky/water/hills) are drawn larger, more opaque, and
pinned to one solid red, so the bridge silhouette reads unambiguously as the
bridge; the sky and water tiers keep their real sampled color, soft, as
atmosphere. The field stays hidden while the ascii portrait is on screen and
materializes, particle by particle, as the page is scrolled down toward the
entries, so the two never overlap. Used as the ambient background on
index.html and diary.html.

## Portrait
`assets/images/ryan.png` — personal photo, resampled client-side (script.js)
