KRAS G12D cryptic-pocket benchmark

sole author · w/ prof. se-jin hwang · CJSJ quarterfinalist, under review

i tested 3 MD-free pocket finders on the cancer drug pocket behind MRTX1133. plain geometry won on the open structure (MCC 0.95); the GNN built for cryptic pockets came last (0.12).

next: does that hold across 15–25 cryptic pockets, and how much do static predictions shift as a pocket opens? then a predictor that reads a cheap ensemble of conformations instead of one snapshot. target: icml & neurips 2027 workshops.
