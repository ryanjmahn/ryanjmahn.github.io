KRAS G12D cryptic-pocket benchmark

sole author · w/ prof. se-jin hwang · CJSJ quarterfinalist, under review

i benchmarked 3 pocket finders on the kras g12d drug pocket and found the cryptic-pocket specialist doesn’t beat plain geometry — fpocket hits 0.95 mcc on the open structure.

now scaled across 218 cryptobench targets: fpocket’s site detection flips on 46% of a conformational subset under <1.4 å of backbone motion. next: a rule for when to trust a prediction. target: icml 2027 workshops.
