Data Readiness in Medical Imaging Benchmarks

co-author · neurips 2026 · AI data readiness for scientific discovery (AIDaR) workshop

audited DermaMNIST, a widely used medical AI dataset, and found its images lose most of what dermatologists diagnose by before training even starts — and lose it unevenly across skin tones. built a format that keeps those signs.

4 of 5 diagnostic signs destroyed at 224×224 → 28×28 · ~80% of detail kept instead of a third · skin-tone gap cut 52%

Benchmark Design in Cryptic Pocket Prediction

sole author · w/ prof. se-jin hwang · CJSJ quarterfinalist, under review

benchmarked three computational tools on their ability to find the Switch-II cryptic pocket in KRAS G12D; the one built for cryptic pockets placed last. two follow-up studies ask what such a leaderboard really measures, and when a prediction is worth believing.

0.95 MCC for the simplest tool vs 0.12 for the specialist · scaled to 218 proteins · a trust rule right 75% of the time against 37% overall · target: icml & iclr 2027 workshops
