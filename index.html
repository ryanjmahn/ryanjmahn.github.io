Data Readiness in Medical Imaging Benchmarks2026

co-author · neurips 2026 · AI data readiness for scientific discovery (AIDaR) workshop

audited dermamnist, a widely used medical ai benchmark, and showed its 28×28 downsampling erases 4 of 5 diagnostic patterns in skin-lesion images, and does so unevenly across skin tones. built a higher-fidelity format that preserves them.

Benchmark Design in Cryptic Pocket Prediction2026

sole author · w/ prof. se-jin hwang · CJSJ semi-finalist, under review

benchmarked three pocket-detection tools on the switch-ii cryptic pocket of kras g12d, using a 24-conformer normal-mode ladder built from the crystal structure. the one designed specifically for cryptic pockets placed last. scaled the study to 218 targets to ask when a prediction is worth believing.

0.95 MCC for the simplest tool vs 0.12 for the specialist · scaled to 218 proteins · a trust rule right 75% of the time against 37% overall
