    problem
    PDAC tumors are small and low-contrast against the tissue around them, so outlining them by hand is slow and varies from reader to reader.

    KRAS G12D cryptic-pocket prediction

    problem
    KRAS G12D is a common driver mutation in pancreatic cancer, and KRAS has few obvious drug-binding sites; cryptic pockets, which open only transiently, are one way in.
    method
    A benchmarking study of cryptic-pocket prediction methods on KRAS G12D.
